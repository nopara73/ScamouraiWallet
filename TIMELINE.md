# Timeline

This timeline is an index, not a verdict. Its **status** column identifies what each source can establish. “Public statement” means only that the statement was made; “author account” marks first-person recollection or attribution; “government representation” and “defense assertion” preserve the position of that party; “indictment allegation” is not a finding; and “adjudicated outcome” is limited to the plea or sentence actually entered. Claim IDs resolve to the corresponding row in [CLAIMS.jsonl](CLAIMS.jsonl).

## Date conventions

- Exact dates use ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`; month-only events use `YYYY-MM`.
- A slash marks an inclusive event interval or two related dates, not uncertainty.
- When a later filing reports an earlier message without giving its exact day, the timeline uses the narrowest supported period and says so.
- Publication, event, filing, plea, and sentence dates are kept distinct. For example, the July 30 pleas were announced by the Justice Department on August 6.
