# Wasabi Research Club transcript archive

YouTube playlist: <https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLPwc75tPMdsgTYlu9dJZlosCm0s7WmIpF>

Historical session notes: <https://github.com/zkSNACKs/WasabiResearchClub>

Retrieved on 2026-07-11. The playlist contained 35 positions: 33 transcripts were available and 2 were unavailable.

- `transcripts/` contains readable Markdown, grouped into roughly 30-second timestamped blocks.
- `raw-json/` preserves the exact timestamped snippets returned by YouTube's transcript interface.
- `raw-vtt/` preserves YouTube's original WebVTT caption downloads.
- `metadata/` preserves yt-dlp metadata for each accessible playlist entry.
- `manifest.json` is the machine-readable completeness report.
- [`SAMOURAI-FINDINGS.md`](SAMOURAI-FINDINGS.md) indexes the passages most relevant to Samourai, SharedCoin, OXT, Whirlpool, and the essay's xpub argument.

YouTube's automatic captions can contain transcription errors. These files preserve the source text; they are not silently corrected.

The current playlist contains later talks and livestreams in addition to the numbered Research Club sessions. This archive follows playlist position, so position 29 is Research Club episode 28 after the private entry at position 28.
